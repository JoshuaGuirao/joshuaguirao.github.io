About Me

I'm an aspiring Software Engineer and Data Scientist pursuing a dual degree in Computer Science and Mathematics at Tufts University, with a minor in AI Development. I'm drawn to problems where solid engineering and rigorous math meet, and I enjoy turning messy, complex problems into systems that are clean, reliable, and useful.

My work spans the full stack. I've built real-time computer vision applications that run pose inference alongside a live web frontend, integrated and stress-tested social media APIs for a production analytics platform, and engineered data pipelines that turn tens of thousands of inconsistent records into analysis-ready datasets.

I'm especially interested in machine learning, computer vision, and data-intensive systems: how models are built, how they're deployed, and how the infrastructure underneath them stays fast and dependable. My background in probability and statistics shapes how I approach data, and I'm always looking to go deeper on the fundamentals behind the tools I use.

Beyond the screen, I'm always eager to collaborate on projects that blend creativity, technical depth, and social good. Let's build something innovative together.
