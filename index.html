About

I'm studying Computer Science and Mathematics at Tufts University, with a minor in AI Development. I'm drawn to problems where solid engineering meets rigorous math, and I like turning messy, complex problems into systems that are clean and reliable.

I've built a real-time computer vision fitness trainer, hardened API integrations for a production analytics platform, and engineered pipelines that turn tens of thousands of messy records into analysis-ready data. At EY-Parthenon, I evaluated software companies' products and tech stacks on PE-backed M&A deals, which taught me to ask not only whether a system works, but why it was built that way and what it's worth.

I work by breaking a problem into well-scoped components with clear interfaces, building and testing each one in isolation, then composing them back into a single system that's easier to debug and more robust than a monolith.

Outside of code, I'm usually lifting, playing chess or piano, cooking Asian food, or following the Toronto Raptors.
