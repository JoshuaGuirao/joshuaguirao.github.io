About Me

I'm an aspiring Software Engineer and Data Scientist pursuing a dual degree in Computer Science and Mathematics at Tufts University, with a minor in AI Development. I'm drawn to problems where solid engineering and rigorous math meet, and I enjoy turning messy, complex problems into systems that are clean, reliable, and useful.

I've worked with technology from two sides: building it and evaluating it. On the building side, I've developed a real-time computer vision fitness trainer that runs pose inference alongside a live web frontend, integrated and stress-tested social media APIs for a production analytics platform, and engineered data pipelines that turn tens of thousands of inconsistent records into analysis-ready datasets. On the evaluating side, I worked on PE-backed software M&A deals at EY-Parthenon, digging into target companies' products and tech stacks to understand how they were built and what made them valuable. Together, those experiences taught me to ask not just whether a system works, but why it was designed that way and what it's worth to the people who rely on it.

My approach sits where software engineering and data science overlap. I start by decomposing a problem into well-scoped components with clear inputs, outputs, and interfaces, whether that's isolating real-time model inference from a web server in separate processes or splitting a messy dataset into cleaning, normalization, and fuzzy-matching stages. Each component gets built, tested, and tuned in isolation, then composed back into a single pipeline. Working at that level of granularity makes failures easier to trace and tradeoffs easier to measure, and the system that comes out the other end is more robust than anything I could have designed as one monolithic piece.

Outside of code, I'm usually lifting, playing chess or piano, cooking Asian food, or following the Toronto Raptors.

I'm looking for opportunities in software engineering, data science, and machine learning where I can keep building, keep learning from people who push me, and work on problems that matter. If that sounds like something you're working on, I'd love to connect.
